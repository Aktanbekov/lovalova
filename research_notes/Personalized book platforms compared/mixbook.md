# Mixbook (mixbook.com) — Business & Product Profile

Researched 2026-11-16. The live site was read on 2026-11-16, during a Black Friday / holiday promo window, so promo banners reflect that window. I did not create an account, upload photos, or enter the editor beyond public pages. No screenshots were taken; findings about the site come from text extraction (WebFetch).

## Site structure & customer funnel

### Takeaway
Mixbook runs a classic e-commerce photo-product funnel: a promo-led homepage, then a theme or format pick, then the editor, then ordering. Two creation paths now sit on top: "Mixbook Studio", a web editor with Auto-Create and Auto-Layout, and "Story Mode", an AI flow driven by a conversation (iOS first). A large sitewide discount (up to 50% off), countdown timers, and social-proof claims ("#1 rated", "6 million+ happy Mixbookers") appear everywhere.

## Product catalog

### Takeaway
Mixbook offers four book formats (Softcover, Hardcover, Layflat, Luxury Album) in square, landscape, and portrait sizes from 6x6" to 14x11". There are three paper families and up to about 400 pages. Around the books sit cards, calendars, wall decor, yearbooks, magazines, and digital outputs (movies, Memory Art). Mixbook has **no personalized character or name-based storybook product**. Its "kids" books are photo books of the child's own photos.

### Inferences
- Mixbook competes with the personalized-storybook players (Wonderbly, Hooray Heroes, Lovebook, Weeloom Tales) only indirectly. Its value is "your real photos plus your story," not "your child as an illustrated character in a pre-written plot." Story Mode's AI-generated captions and narrative layouts do move it closer to storytelling. An AI "storybook from your photos" product would be a natural extension, but I found no evidence that one exists (inference).
- "Board Book," "ABC Book," and themed kids collections (Crayola, Oh Joy!) show demand for children's keepsakes that Mixbook serves with templates rather than generated stories.

### Gaps
- I found no cover upgrade options or prices (photo-wrap vs. linen/leather), no paper-upgrade prices, and no numeric paper weights on public pages; these likely appear only inside the editor or at checkout.

## AI & technology

### Takeaway
Mixbook has added AI features steadily:
- 2023: ML-driven Mixbook Studio with Auto-Create and Auto-Layout.
- 2024: Mixbook Movies, which auto-generates a video from a photo book order.
- June 2026: Story Mode, a conversation-based AI photo book generator that writes captions, picks hero photos, and produces three drafts.
- September 2026: a free iOS "memories" app with dedupe, timelines, Stories, and Mixbook+.

The company describes its AI as "patent-pending memory technology."

### Cited Findings
- 2023: Mixbook Studio launched "using machine learning." 2024: **Mixbook Movies** launched, generating a shareable video from a photo book order. 2026: **Story Mode** launched. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixbook)
- **Story Mode** launched on June 11, 2026, described as "patent-pending AI technology for memory understanding and photobook generation."
  - Steps: (1) describe the occasion in conversation; (2) set style and storytelling preferences, from photo-forward to "full narrative storytelling"; (3) upload photos; (4) choose a design style; (5) receive print-ready options.
  - Layout logic: milestone moments get full-page spreads, supporting photos are grouped for pacing, and generated captions add context and emotion. Everything stays editable.

  — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mixbook-launches-story-mode-turning-a-few-words-into-a-finished-photo-book-302797383.html)

Claimed results: "3.5X faster" creation and time to order "cut in half." These are company claims. — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mixbook-launches-story-mode-turning-a-few-words-into-a-finished-photo-book-302797383.html)
- **Platforms**:
  - Story Mode launched on iOS first; ProVideoCoalition says it also runs on iPadOS and on Apple Silicon Macs.

### Inferences
- Mixbook's AI strategy differs from the storybook companies. It uses AI to (a) cut the effort of curating and laying out real photos, which is the main abandonment point, and (b) add narrative text. It does not use AI to generate illustrated characters or images. Positioning itself around memory science ("The Memory Project") is a brand move toward "storytelling" (inference).
- Running photo analysis on-device on iOS cuts compute and privacy costs and explains the iOS-first rollout (inference).

### Gaps
- I found no public detail on which LLM vendor Mixbook uses or on its web tech stack.
- I could not confirm whether AI image enhancement or upscaling is automatic or opt-in, or whether generative image features exist (none found).

## Business (founding, funding, scale, manufacturing, reputation)

Mixbook is a private company founded in 2006 by UC Berkeley students Andrew Laffoon (CEO) and Aryk Grosz. It is headquartered in Redwood City, CA (earlier Palo Alto). It was lightly funded ($10M from Level Equity in 2011, the only publicly known round) and claims 6M+ customers and 20M+ projects. Revenue and headcount estimates vary widely, and I could not verify any in-house printing plant.
