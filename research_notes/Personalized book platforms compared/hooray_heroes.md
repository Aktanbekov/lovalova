# Hooray Heroes (hoorayheroes.com): business and product profile

Researched 16 Nov 2026. Primary method: live browsing of hoorayheroes.com (US store) in a browser, including the full builder for "The Dream Team for 2 Kids" up to the cart (no checkout, account or payment). Also used Shopify's public `/products.json` catalogue feed, the site's policy pages, Trustpilot and press coverage. Facts marked "observed in builder" come from my own browsing session on 16 Nov 2026 and have no other public URL. The relevant product URL is given instead.

**Headline correction to the brief:** Hooray Heroes is **not Dutch**. It was founded in **Slovenia in 2013**. On about 6–7 Oct 2026 it was **acquired by Personalized Story Group Inc. (PSG)**, a Delaware corporation. PSG now runs the US site on Shopify.

## 1. Site structure and customer funnel

### Takeaway
This is a classic Shopify DTC funnel built around a modal "Hero Palette" avatar builder. The flow is: product page → "Preview Full Book" → one modal step per character (gender + name, then appearance) → language and story selection → dedication → hardcover upsell → full-book flip preview → cart. Prices are anchored on permanent strike-through discounts. Social proof leans on "3 million books sold", review counts and "As seen on" logos.

Checkout options: Shop Pay, PayPal, G Pay. "Taxes and shipping calculated at checkout."
- No account or login is needed before checkout. There was **no photo upload** step, and the cart showed **no gift-wrap or plush upsell** for this title. Gift wraps and plushies exist as separate products (see §2).
- The FAQ confirms the self-serve approach: you personalize the characters yourself, so "there is no need to send them [photos] in advance!" — [FAQ](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/faq)

Customers can also choose and order stories and write a dedication — [About Us](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/about-company)

**Urgency, discounts and social proof**
- Every book shows a permanent compare-at price: $59.99 → $39.99 (softcover) and $69.99 → $49.98 or $49.99 (hardcover). There was no sitewide sale banner on the homepage — [hoorayheroes.com](https://hoorayheroes.com); [products.json feed](https://hoorayheroes.com/products.json)
- FAQ discount code: "The code 70PLUS (for orders above $70) will get you 20% off all books in your cart." — [FAQ](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/faq)
- "Free Shipping Offer: Purchase 3 or more books and enjoy complimentary shipping for a limited time." — [Shipping, Payment and Returns](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/shipping-payment-and-return)
- Premium editions use scarcity copy: "Limited Edition – only 10,000 copies available!" and "Materials are limited… order now." They also claim "USA's #1 love book for 5 years running" — [all products](https://hoorayheroes.com/collections/all)
- Celebrity claims: the books "moved… famous names like Khloé Kardashian, David Beckham, and Gigi Hadid" — [About Us](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/about-company)

### Inferences
- The funnel puts all personalization before price friction: the hardcover upsell and price appear only after the preview. The preview is the emotional peak, and it sits directly before "Add to cart".
- The "Customize Book" step says it "preselected the best options to make checkout faster". Defaults are tuned to cut the number of decisions, and story curation is optional and hidden under "Advanced".
- Organising the navigation by number of characters (1–4 kids, family of 3/4/5) is a structural differentiator against single-child-focused competitors such as Wonderbly.

### Gaps
- I did not enter checkout, so the post-cart upsells (if any), the checkout shipping selector and the email-capture behaviour were not observed.
- I did not count the exact number of hair styles in the carousel.
- I did not check whether any title offers a photo upload (the Terms reference one; see §4).

## 2. Product catalogue, format and design

### Takeaway
There are about **42 purchasable personalized book titles**, out of 134 "Personalized Book" SKUs in a 294-item Shopify catalogue. Many listings are "COMING SOON" or legacy name-specific pages. The books are landscape **12 × 8.5 in, ~28 pages, softcover or hardcover**, with 10 swappable "stories" (episodes) chosen from a pool. The art is a polished, Pixar-like cartoon style drawn in-house. Side lines are puzzles, greeting cards, plush toys, gift boxes, newspaper "life chronicle" books and free printables.

- Many legacy "COMING SOON" listings named after example children ("10 Reasons Dominica Loves Mommy", "Ava's First ABCs") look like SEO landing pages imported during the Shopify migration rather than live products.
- The illustration style, seen in screenshots, is glossy, rounded, big-eyed "3D-look" digital painting in the Pixar/DreamWorks manner, with variant layers per attribute.

### Gaps
- I verified page count and trim size only for Dream Team. Other titles may differ, and baby books are likely shorter.
- I found no posters on the US store.

## 4. AI and technology

### Takeaway
The public brand position is explicitly **anti-AI**: "No AI - No Shortcuts… conceived, written, designed, and produced by talented humans". Personalization is a **pre-drawn layered avatar system** ("Hero Palette™") with no photo upload in the observed builder. However, the post-acquisition **Terms of Service (Oct/Nov 2026) add clauses for photo uploads and "Generated Artwork"** produced by "contracted technology and service providers", which suggests photo-to-character products are being readied. A Trustpilot review also alleges AI imagery in the newspaper books.

### Cited Findings
- Homepage: "No AI - No Shortcuts. All Hooray Heroes personalized books and products are conceived, written, designed, and produced by talented humans with an eye for detail." — [hoorayheroes.com](https://hoorayheroes.com)
- The personalizer is branded "Hero Palette™", with options for skin tone, hair, eyes, glasses and freckles — [hoorayheroes.com](https://hoorayheroes.com)
- "Our team of illustrators and computer programmers continue to dedicate themselves to extending our offer" — [About Us](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/about-company)
- The careers page (legacy) lists the tech stack as "PHP/Laravel and JavaScript/Vue" — [Careers](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/career)
- The current US store runs on **Shopify** ("Our online store is hosted on Shopify"), with Shop Pay and PayPal — [Terms](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/terms-and-conditions)
- Terms §8, "Customer Content and Photo Uploads": "'Customer Content' means photographs, names, text…" Uploaders must warrant that they are the parent or guardian of children pictured — [Terms](https://hoorayheroes.com/policies/terms-of-service)
- Terms §10, "Image Processing and Generated Artwork":
  - "We use contracted technology and service providers to process Customer Content and create illustrations, avatars, previews, and other personalized artwork ('Generated Artwork')."
  - Providers are not authorised "to train or improve their general-purpose or foundation artificial intelligence models".
  - "Generated Artwork is created using automated and artistic processes. It may not be an exact reproduction of the person or photograph provided."
  - Source: [Terms](https://hoorayheroes.com/policies/terms-of-service)
- Terms §4 prohibits using "Hooray Heroes content, artwork, characters, generated outputs… to train… an artificial intelligence or machine-learning model" — [Terms](https://hoorayheroes.com/policies/terms-of-service)
- The privacy policy was last updated 31 Oct 2026 — [Privacy policy](https://hoorayheroes.com/policies/privacy-policy)
- A Trustpilot review (Nov 2026) says a newspaper-style book used AI-generated images instead of real event photos. This is one review and unverified — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hoorayheroes.com)
- PSG says it plans to invest in "new titles, expanded personalization capabilities and additional international growth" — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/personalized-story-group-acquires-hooray-heroes/); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/160306-personalized-story-group-buys-slovenias-hooray-heroes-to-expand-publishi/)

### Inferences
- The Terms are a group-wide template written for PSG's portfolio (Story Bug, My Story Tale, Wall Art Prints), which likely includes photo-based or AI-generated products. Their presence on hoorayheroes.com most likely shows platform consolidation, not current Hooray Heroes AI use. It also leaves room for a photo-to-avatar feature on Hooray Heroes. There is a visible tension between the homepage "No AI" claim and the "Generated Artwork" terms. Competitors could exploit this, or Hooray could drop the claim.
- The Newspaper books (an "Adult" category) are the most likely place where generated or stock imagery is used.

### Gaps
- I found no public statement from Hooray Heroes or PSG announcing an AI or photo-upload launch through Nov 2026.
- I could not verify whether Story Bug or My Story Tale use AI. The search turned up no reliable source.

## 5. Business: ownership, scale, fulfilment and marketing

### Takeaway
Founded in **Slovenia in 2013** by Mic Melanšek and Rado Daradan, Hooray Heroes grew to 100+ staff and 3M+ books sold across about 15 markets. Its English-speaking markets (US, CA, UK, AU since 2018) make up 49% of books sold. In **October 2026 it was acquired by Personalized Story Group (PSG; CEO Brendon Watson)**, now the US operator ("Personalized Story Group Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as Hooray Heroes"). US books are printed domestically. The About page names Utah, Texas and Nebraska; the newer Shipping page says New Jersey. Trustpilot shows 4.3 from about 7.5k reviews.

## 6. Design, brand and mobile

### Takeaway
The site is a bright, family-friendly Shopify theme: white background, orange primary CTA, pink/magenta builder buttons and blue accent bars. Fonts are Lato (body), Poppins (builder UI) and Caveat (handwritten accents). The tone is warm and emotional ("tears of joy", "Hooray!"). Mobile is a single-column, card-carousel layout with a hamburger menu.

### Cited Findings
- Body font Lato, headings Lato, plus Poppins and Caveat on the page. Body text color rgb(94,105,119), heading color rgb(40,46,60), white background. The "CREATE YOURS HERE" button is orange rgb(236,160,73). Top bar is blue ("Ships from USA"). Builder CTAs and the "Preview Full Book" button are pink (observed via computed styles and screenshots) — [hoorayheroes.com](https://hoorayheroes.com)
- The logo is an orange hand-lettered "Hooray Heroes" wordmark — [hoorayheroes.com](https://hoorayheroes.com)
- Tone-of-voice examples: "Some readers laugh, some (most!) cry tears of joy – create yours now and let the emotions begin!" and "Hooray Heroes Land is a magical place…" — [product page](https://hoorayheroes.com/products/personalized-gift-for-two-children); [Careers](https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/career)
- **Mobile at 375px** (observed): blue "Ships from USA" bar, hamburger + logo + search + cart header, a full-width collage hero of book covers with an orange "CREATE YOURS HERE" pill, and "Your favorites" as a horizontal swipe carousel. Each card has a badge ("MEMORIAL BOOK"), stars and review count, struck price and "CREATE HERE" button. Then "MORE BOOKS" and "AS SEEN ON" logos — [hoorayheroes.com](https://hoorayheroes.com)

### Inferences
- The design is competent but generic Shopify. The differentiation is in the illustration quality and the multi-character builder, not the web design.
- The builder is a modal over the product page, and works on mobile as a full-height sheet. Its swatch-and-thumbnail pattern needs no typing beyond names, which suits thumb-driven paid-social traffic.

### Gaps
